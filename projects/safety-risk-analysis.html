Safety overview

This dashboard summarizes weekly workplace incidents and incident rates for FY2025, alongside the recorded root causes, severity codes, and contributing factors. The trend charts show how incident frequency changes over time; the category charts show the distribution within each source dataset.

What the analysis shows

Weekly incident counts

The site series contains 203 incidents across 52 fiscal weeks. Week 29 has the highest count at 14, followed by week 10 at 10 and week 32 at 9. Across 1.65 million exposure hours, the overall rate is 24.58 incidents per 200,000 hours.

Incidents by process path

The process-path records contain 201 incidents. Pick accounts for 49 and Stow to Prime for 41, together representing 44.8% of that total. The ten paths with the highest counts account for 163 incidents, or 81.1%.

Recorded root causes

Of 199 root-cause records, 57 are labeled “Missing standards or procedures” and 36 “Inadequate implementation of procedure.” The five largest categories total 143 records, or 71.9%, including 13 labeled “Unspecified.”

Severity codes

The severity snapshot contains 150 incidents coded D and 49 coded C, totaling 199. These are the source’s category labels; the data supplied here does not define their severity meaning.

Contributing factors

The factor snapshot contains 18 records: 12 Human – Behaviour, 3 Organizational – Process, 2 Human – Leadership, and 1 Organizational – Workplace.

How the summaries relate

The site, process-path, root-cause, severity, and factor files have different totals. Each chart describes its own records; the category totals are not added to the site total.
