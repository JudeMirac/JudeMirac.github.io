What the analysis shows

Service performance varies

Overall on-time performance is 57.4%. Contractor results range from 46.0% to 62.0%, with Liberty Transit Services recording the lowest rate and 32 late pickups.

Some trip records are incomplete

Six completed trips are missing an actual pickup time. Across all trips, 12 records are missing a vehicle assignment and 9 are missing a driver assignment. A trip can have more than one missing field.

Fleet availability adds context

The fleet snapshot shows 3 of 56 vehicles out of service. The maintenance records contain 42 mechanical-failure events.
