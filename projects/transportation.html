What the analysis shows

Three priorities for review

01 / Service reliability

Start with the largest gap

Liberty Transit Services has the lowest OTP at 46.0% and the most late pickups at 32. Compare routes and time periods before choosing an intervention; the aggregate result alone does not establish a cause.

02 / Data quality

Separate missing data from service status

There are 94 missing actual pickup times, but only 6 belong to completed trips. Also review the 12 missing vehicle assignments and 9 missing driver assignments. Missing fields can overlap on a single trip.

03 / Fleet & compliance

Keep readiness visible

The fleet table records 3 of 56 vehicles out of service. There are 42 mechanical-failure maintenance events and 12 overdue open corrective actions as of September 24, 2026. Compliance does not affect the OTP risk label.

Data model & validation

Use the right unit of analysis

Trips form the service-performance table. Contractors provide names and grouping. Drivers and vehicles support assignment checks. Maintenance and compliance remain at their own event and audit grain to avoid multiplying trip counts.

Trip-level dashboard exports omit driver names and license numbers. The original CSVs remain the source of record.

Reconcile before presenting

Validated primary keys and trip references, checked date parsing, reconciled contractor subtotals, and tested the −5, +15, and +16 minute boundaries. OTP uses weighted trip counts rather than an average of contractor percentages.

Fleet status is the supplied table snapshot. Maintenance and audit dates have their own coverage; they are not described as trip-period measures.
