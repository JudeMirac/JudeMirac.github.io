03 / Decision modelingPython · scikit-learn

Fraud Risk Modeling

Turn ranked transaction scores into an actionable policy that balances fraud capture and manual review capacity.

61%of fraud captured through
review or blocking in the project
Allow 80%
Approach & findings

Compared Logistic Regression and Random Forest, used class weighting for an imbalanced target, and engineered interaction features from transaction behavior.

Percentile tiers assign the bottom 80% to ALLOW, the next 15% to REVIEW, and the top 5% to BLOCK. The 61% capture result is a project evaluation, not a production outcome.
